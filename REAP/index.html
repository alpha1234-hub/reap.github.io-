How We Work

Our name describes our cycle of innovation, carrying projects from theoretical discovery through to reliable deployment.

Full-Cycle Pipeline

From literature review to production deployment, in one continuous, self-improving loop.

R

Research

We discover foundational principles, explore advanced deep learning architectures, and solve mathematical boundary constraints for vision and medical tasks.

E

Evaluation

Every breakthrough is subjected to rigorous, uncertainty-aware testing, benchmarking, and statistical checks to guarantee real-world safety.

A

Agents

We create dynamic, autonomous agent architectures capable of understanding complex user intents and acting inside automated environments.

P

Production

We deploy optimized, low-latency codebases tailored for edge systems, from clinical MRI scanners to real-time train locomotive safety controllers.

Recent Projects

Explore some of our published research contributions in medical diagnostics, railway safety, and image processing.

Medical MRI Frontiers in Radiology (2025)

Ultra-lightweight uncertainty-aware ensemble for large-scale multi-class medical MRI diagnosis

An extremely compact diagnostic ensemble using confidence-weighted experts and variance-based uncertainty estimations to support rural clinics on low-resource hardware.

Authors: Sowad Rahman, Fahmid Al Farid, Mahe Zabin, Jia Uddin, Hezerul Abdul Karim
Kidney Disease Frontiers in Big Data (2026)

When uncertainty guides learning: a highly effective approach to kidney disease classification in CT imaging

Active learning method leveraging entropy-based uncertainty to selectively sample high-value CT scans, reducing label acquisition costs while classifying Normal, Stone, Cyst, and Tumors.

Authors: Muslima Akter, Fahmid Al Farid, Md Yousuf Ahmad, Md Azad Hossain Raju, Sowad Rahman, Jia Uddin, Hezerul Bin Abdul Karim
Machine Vision Railway Safety Division

Automated Train Control Using Machine Vision for Red Flag Detection in Railway Safety

Locomotive autopilot protection combining convolutional neural network detectors and active brake triggers to alert operators and auto-stop trains upon recognizing red flag hazards.

Authors: REAP Engineering Group, Deep Learning Collaborators
Image Restorations Adaptive Attention

Multi-Scale Feature Fusion with Adaptive Attention for Robust Image Deblurring

An image enhancement network combining multi-scale resolutions and channel/spatial adaptive attention masks to suppress artifacts and restore clarity in motion-degraded photos.

Authors: REAP Engineering Group, Vision Science Division
Medical Imaging Biomedical Signal Processing (2025)

Attention-guided U-Net ensemble for automated spleen segmentation in abdominal CT

A boundary-refining segmentation network using attention gates and multi-scale skip connections to precisely delineate spleen contours for splenomegaly screening.

Authors: Sowad Rahman, REAP Research Group
Autonomous Agents REAP Labs — Internal Systems

Multi-agent orchestration framework for autonomous research pipeline automation

A planner-executor-evaluator agent loop that retrieves literature, runs experiments, and reports evaluation metrics with minimal human supervision, closing the loop between research and evaluation.

Authors: REAP Engineering Group, Agent Systems Division
